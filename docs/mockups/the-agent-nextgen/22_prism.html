erpdb-reconciliation-recipes

Shipped with the product. Available to every tenant; read-only.

Reconciling ERPDB invoices against purchase orders: 1. Join invoices → purchase_orders on po_number, not vendor name. 2. Vendor names drift; always resolve through vendors.canonical_id first. 3. A 3-way match needs the receipt line too — wms.receipts on po_line_id. 4. Tolerance is 2% or $50, whichever is smaller. Anything outside is an exception, not a rounding difference. 5. Report exceptions with the PO, the invoice, and the delta. Never net them off.
