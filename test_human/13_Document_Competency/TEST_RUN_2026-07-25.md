   extraction through the throttled proxy. Full 18-doc / 264-page corpus ≈ **100 minutes
   wall-clock**. Direct input for the re-core economics (batching/caching candidates).

   fully-flattened, merged, digital — with zero blank-stored pages and zero AI-routing of
   normal text pages (no cost added where nothing was wrong).

   job's `ArchiveDirectory` — `make_fixtures.py` is idempotent for re-runs; the runner also
   falls back to `fixtures/_archived/` for knowledge attachment.
