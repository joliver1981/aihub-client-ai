How this works

These settings control the email the platform sends — batch notifications, review reminders, workflow alerts. Saving here overrides the server's .env values and takes effect on the next email, no restart. Passwords and connection strings are stored in the machine's encrypted secrets store and are never shown back. (Inbound email processing is configured elsewhere and is not affected.)
